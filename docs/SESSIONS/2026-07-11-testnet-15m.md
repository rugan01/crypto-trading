## Findings and changes

The first IOC attempt at displayed best bids received `order_size_not_available_in_orderbook` for both legs despite large public L2 sizes. No position was created. A bounded 5%-through-bid retry filled at the displayed best bids. Testnet displayed depth therefore cannot be assumed executable.

Telegram notifications were synchronous during this run and delayed stop arming by about one second. Notifications are now dispatched outside the risk thread. This change requires a regression test before the next session.

The high REST-recovery share shows that the stream needs further tuning and explicit per-symbol freshness tracking. The fallback prevented a monitoring gap, but routine REST usage at this level is not the desired steady state.

## Unvalidated paths

- This demo result says nothing about production fills or liquidity.
